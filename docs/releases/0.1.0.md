Verdra 0.1.0 is the first preview, for the owner and invited testers. It changes how Roblox looks
on your own computer only: other players see the game as usual.

## What works

- **Replace a picture or a mesh** in a Roblox game with another Roblox asset (Asset ID), a file
  from your computer (Local file: PNG, JPEG, KTX2 or DDS pictures; Roblox mesh files or OBJ), or
  an HTTPS link (URL), or make it disappear (Remove). Tested in game on Windows: pictures near and
  far, with transparency; meshes from an OBJ file, from an asset ID, and Remove.
- **Profiles:** group replacements and turn them on and off. **Apply now** puts them to work.
- **Nothing hidden, nothing permanent:** Settings › System changes lists everything Verdra
  changed outside its own folders, and **Reset everything** undoes all of it.

## Known limits

- **Windows only.** Linux and macOS are paused, planned later.
- **Not signed yet.** The first time you start it, Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected
  your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **A replaced mesh keeps the part's own texture.** Verdra replaces the shape only; the texture
  is a separate asset and is drawn on the new shape. Replace the texture too if you want a
  different one.
- **Roblox Player only**, started through Verdra. Not Roblox Studio.
- Sounds, animations and other asset types from a file or link aren't supported yet, nor
  TexturePack slots. A mesh is sent with one level of detail.
- If Roblox asks for an asset by its content hash, Verdra can't replace it yet; Activity says so.

## Install

1. Download **Verdra-0.1.0-windows.zip** below.
2. Right-click it › **Extract All…** and keep the folder Windows suggests
   (`Verdra-0.1.0-windows`), or move that folder somewhere you keep.
3. Open its **Verdra** folder and double-click **verdra.exe**.

To check the download, compare its SHA-256 with **SHA256SUMS** (in PowerShell:
`Get-FileHash Verdra-0.1.0-windows.zip`).

## Update or reset

- **Update:** quit Verdra (tray icon › Quit Verdra), delete the Verdra folder and extract the new
  version in its place. Settings and profiles in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Verdra` are kept.
- **Reset:** in Verdra, **Settings › System changes › Reset everything…** removes everything
  Verdra changed on this computer. Do it before deleting the folder if you stop using Verdra.

## Privacy

No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports. Verdra talks to Roblox (only what your Roblox
client asks for, and the asset lookups you start) and to the links you enter. Everything else
stays on your computer. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/verdra-1/verdra/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
