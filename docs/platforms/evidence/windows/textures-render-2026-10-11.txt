Verdra texture test on the maintainer's PC, 11 October 2026 (source run).
Command: run-latest.ps1 --format-check 15553230204 --format-capture <a public mesh ID>
Anonymised: no user name, no place, universe or session IDs, no CDN addresses or content
hashes, no content bytes. Asset IDs are public Toolbox assets.

Seen in game (screenshots and videos kept by the maintainer, not in the repository)
- Local file, PNG with transparency, served as BC3 384 x 384: drawn, transparency right.
- Local file, opaque PNG 576 x 512 and 1024 x 1024, served as BC1: drawn.
- Link (an HTTPS picture), served as BC1 832 x 576: drawn.
- Remove, served as a transparent BC3 4 x 4: the decal is gone.
- Verdra's contentHash (MD5 of its level) differs from what Roblox computes; Roblox drew the
  texture anyway, so the client doesn't check it.
- Remaining fault: the replaced texture is black at a distance and on first sight, and turns
  right up close; a sharp boundary moves across the wall as the player walks up.

Format check reports for 15553230204 (500 x 500 with alpha), without a replacement
Roblox asked the batch for the asset three times, once per fidelity, each with its own CDN
address, and downloaded all three within a second:
  fidelity "/////AE=" (bytes ff ff ff fc 01): KTX2 BC3 28 x 28, 5 levels,
    uncompressed level lengths 784, 256, 64, 16, 16 (28, 14, 7, 3, 1 pixels a side),
    DFD at offset 200; level data stored smallest first right after the key/value data
  fidelity "/////AI=" (... fc 02): KTX2 BC3 224 x 224, 3 levels (50176, 12544, 3136),
    DFD at offset 152
  fidelity "/////AM=" (... fc 03): KTX2 BC3 448 x 448, 1 level (200704), DFD at offset 104
The keys of the full one (the 8 October capture): packIndex "2", pack_0 "4,5", pack_1 "1,3",
pack_2 "0,1": each pack's first mip level and level count. 960 x 640 had pack_0 "4,6".
So the three answers are packs 0, 1 and 2 of one mip chain: pack 2 = level 0, pack 1 =
levels 1 to 3, pack 0 = levels 4 to the last (1 x 1). The last byte of the fidelity is the
pack number plus one in every answer seen.

With a replacement, in every run Roblox asked only for the fidelity "/////AE=" (pack 0) and
Verdra answered it with one full-size level whose packIndex was "2"; no other pack was asked
for. Cause of the black texture: Roblox had no data for the smaller levels.

Mesh capture (a public Toolbox mesh)

- Download: Content-Encoding zstd; the body starts "version 7.00" with a COREMESH chunk; two
  different compressed sizes were seen for the same decoded size.
